Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about browsing, product details and placing an order.

Are the prices final?

Prices are shown according to the current business-provided price list. Please confirm availability and delivery charges before placing an order.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your basket, open the order page, fill in your delivery details and select “Review order on WhatsApp”. Check the prepared message and press Send in WhatsApp.

Are ingredients and allergens confirmed?

Any item marked “Business to confirm” needs verified information from the business and final product label before publication.

Are delivery charges included?

No. Delivery charges and the final order total are confirmed by the business after checking the address and requested products.

How do I add a new product or photo?

Follow the README guide. Add the image under assets/images and add the product entry to js/products.js.
